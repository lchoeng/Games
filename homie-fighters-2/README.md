# Homie Fighters 2

The sequel to [Homie Fighter](https://github.com/lchoeng/homie-fighter): a Street Fighter–style brawler starring Vince, Joe, Tim, Gabe, Glen, Arvin and Loni.

Open `index.html` in a browser (works on phone and desktop).

- Stages: The Park, The Street, Grocery Store
- Desktop controls: arrows move/jump/duck, Z punch, X kick, C special, V shoot, Enter select, M music. Hold away from your opponent to block.
- Phone: on-screen d-pad, Punch, Kick, Shoot and Special buttons.

| Fighter | Special | Shoots |
|---|---|---|
| Vince | Blade Kick | Dumbbell |
| Joe | Pinoy Uppercut | Glock 19 |
| Tim | Hurricane Fists | Golf ball |
| Gabe | Hurricane Elbows | Video game controller |
| Glen | Raging Bicycle Knee | Basketball |
| Arvin | Furious Cartwheel | Running shoes |
| Loni | Helicopter Kick | Cup of boba |
